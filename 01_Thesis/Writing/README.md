# Thesis writing and publication assets

This directory is for **your** approved thesis work, not copied reference reports.

- [`chapters/`](chapters/): original prose and literature notes.
- [`figures/`](figures/): figures with source/generation provenance.
- [`tables/`](tables/): curated tables tied to validated result manifests.

Keep bibliographic records here when verified against original sources. Do not infer author names or publication venues from filenames or PDF tool metadata. Cite papers, code, datasets and model releases as appropriate; their licenses and provenance are distinct.

Every empirical claim must link to a completed experiment under `experiments/` and its matching `results/`/`outputs/`. Every reused figure or substantial code adaptation must retain attribution and satisfy its own license. Reference leaderboard scores remain author-reported until reproduced and are not thesis BHS/CCC measurements.

Study the preserved reports, LaTeX, figures and presentations through [the reuse guide](../docs/RESEARCH_REUSE.md). Use them as methodological references, not as text or results to pass off as your own.
