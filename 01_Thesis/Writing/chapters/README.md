# Original thesis chapters

Suggested working structure, to revise with your supervisor:

1. Introduction: Bengali hallucination detection and the separate dialectal-robustness question.
2. Related work: verified literature and attributed research implementations, including limitations.
3. Data and protocol: schemas, label orientation, source-lineage audit, taxonomy review, dialect annotations and held-out design.
4. Methods: baselines, judge prompts/parsing, any independently tested reused components, inference and checkpointing.
5. Experiments and results: BHS/errors, 12-type breakdown, CoT ablations, weight sensitivity, CCC/CBS and coverage.
6. Discussion: failure analysis, contamination risks, uncertainty, validity threats, efficiency and limitations.
7. Conclusion and future work.

This is a writing outline, not completed prose or a claim that any experiment succeeded. Link methods/results to actual registered experiments. Keep lineage-assisted reproductions clearly separated from independent evaluation.
